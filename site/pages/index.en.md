A record of the playgrounds in City of Vancouver parks, to be searchable by the kind of play equipment.
It is in preparation: for now it lists the 114 parks (136 playgrounds) from the City's open data.
The equipment will be filled in from site visits with photographs.
The first one surveyed is [Wesbrook Park](items/ubc-wesbrook.html) at UBC, which is not in the City's data (October 2026).

Names for play equipment are kept in the [equipment vocabulary](vocab/).
Each park is also available as JSON-LD.
