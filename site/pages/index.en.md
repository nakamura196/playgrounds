A record of the playgrounds in City of Vancouver parks, to be searchable by the kind of play equipment.
It is in preparation: for now it lists the 114 parks (136 playgrounds) from the City's open data, plus 17 City parks missing from its playground list (found in OpenStreetMap, with the playgrounds checked on aerial imagery).
The equipment will be filled in from site visits with photographs.
Parks surveyed on site are listed under "[Recently surveyed](#recent)" below, newest first. The survey started in October 2026 with three parks at UBC, which are not in the City's data.

Names for play equipment are kept in the [equipment vocabulary](vocab/).
Each park is also available as JSON-LD.
