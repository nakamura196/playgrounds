## Park data

All parks as a table.
Click a column name to sort, or type in the box at the top right to filter.
Use "Download Data" at the top right to get everything as CSV or JSON.

Each park is also available as JSON-LD, from the "JSON-LD" button on its page or at `items/<id>.jsonld`.
The equipment vocabulary is at [vocab/vocab.jsonld](vocab/vocab.jsonld) (SKOS).
