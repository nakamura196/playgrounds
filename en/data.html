Park data

All parks as a table. Click a column name to sort, or type in the box at the top right to filter. Use “Download Data” at the top right to get everything as CSV or JSON.

Each park is also available as JSON-LD, from the “JSON-LD” button on its page or at items/<id>.jsonld. The equipment vocabulary is at vocab/vocab.jsonld (SKOS).

Vancouver Playgrounds

A record of the 136 playgrounds in 114 City of Vancouver parks, to be searchable by play equipment. It starts from the City's open data and adds playgrounds surveyed on site.
