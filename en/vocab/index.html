Play equipment vocabulary

Types of play equipment, defined by shape and movement. Product names are not used as type names; products are listed separately and point to a type. "Different from" marks a type that looks similar but is not the same; it is stated in both directions. Correspondence with Chicago Playgrounds is marked "same" only when checked against photographs or descriptions; other links are candidates chosen by name. Definitions are in Japanese for now.
