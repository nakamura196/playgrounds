公園
Brockhouse Park
説明
Splash pad in Brockhouse Park (Wesbrook Village, UBC), next to the community centre: a round pad with boulders set in the concrete and ground spray nozzles, and a water-play channel lined with boulders, with a water wheel. Dry in October. No play structure was seen on the survey day. Not in the City of Vancouver open data; the position is where the survey photos were taken.
