公園
Wesbrook Park
説明
1 playground in Wesbrook Park (Wesbrook Village, UBC), with an outdoor fitness corner (KOMPAN) and a bouldering wall at its south side. Not in the City of Vancouver open data; the position is where the survey photos were taken. The tube slide tower is by Corocord (2019, ages 5-12). Also has a post with three red rings whose use is unknown.
